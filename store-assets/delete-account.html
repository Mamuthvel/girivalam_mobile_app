Delete Your Account & Data

Girivalam app (developer: Girivalam)

You can permanently delete your Girivalam account and the data associated with it at any time. There are two ways to do this.

Option 1 — Delete from within the app

  1. Open the Girivalam app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

Send an email from the address linked to your account to:

support@YOURDOMAIN.com

Use the subject line Delete my Girivalam account. We will verify and process your request within 30 days and confirm by email.

What data is deleted

  • Your account and profile (name, email/phone)
  • Saved locations and your Girivalam journey history
  • Group memberships and in-app messages you sent
  • Any location data associated with your account

What may be retained

  • Records we are legally required to keep (e.g. donation/transaction records), retained only for the period required by law.
  • Anonymised, aggregated analytics that can no longer identify you.

Questions? Contact support@YOURDOMAIN.com.
